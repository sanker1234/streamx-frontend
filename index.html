Disclaimer & Important Guidelines

🛡️
Essential Notice: CineJoy does NOT host or store any media.

Every video stream, iframe embed, audio file, subtitle, and download link accessible through CineJoy is hosted on third-party servers not owned, maintained, or controlled by CineJoy.

1. Automated Content Indexing

CineJoy is an automated client-side web application designed to organize and index publicly accessible metadata from public databases. When media playback is initiated, the application connects to external third-party services and content delivery networks to present streams. CineJoy does not host, manage, or control these external services.

2. Zero Server-Side Media Storage

CineJoy operates without any local or remote file storage, video transcoding servers, or media upload pipelines. CineJoy servers do not hold, upload, copy, or distribute copyrighted video files. No proprietary video files exist on any server operated by CineJoy.

3. Third-Party Content & Links

Any stream playback, advertisements displayed within external player frames, or external download mirrors are strictly the responsibility of the respective third-party providers. CineJoy disclaims all liability regarding the availability, legality, performance, or content provided by external sources.

CineJoy User Guidelines & Recommendations

🛡️
Use Ad & Popup Blockers

External embed servers may display popups or third-party ads. We strongly recommend using uBlock Origin or the Brave Browser for a clean, uninterrupted cinema experience.

⚡
Multi-Server Switching

If you experience slow loading, buffering, or an unavailable stream on Server 1, immediately switch to Server 2 through Server 9 using the server buttons beneath the player.

🌐
DNS & Connection Optimization

For optimal streaming speed and to bypass ISP throttling, consider using privacy-focused DNS services such as Cloudflare 1.1.1.1 or Google Public DNS (8.8.8.8).

CineJoy is developed exclusively for personal, private, and educational media exploration. Redistribution, commercial exploitation, or re-broadcasting is strictly prohibited.

Playback Quality & Audio Modes

Most anime and movie servers support both Subtitled (SUB) and English Dubbed (DUB) audio. Use the Audio Toggle pills in the player control bar to toggle your preferred audio language instantly.

DMCA Notice & Copyright Compliance

CineJoy respects the intellectual property rights of others and adheres to the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and international copyright legislation. Because CineJoy does not host or upload any video files, we cannot delete content from the web.

How to Takedown Content at the Source:
  1. Identify the specific external host or service delivering the content.
  2. Submit a formal DMCA takedown request directly to the responsible host provider. Once removed by the file host, the media will immediately become unplayable across the entire internet, including on CineJoy.
  3. If you wish to request the removal of specific search metadata or indexing links on CineJoy, please send a detailed notice including the exact TMDB / AniList ID and title to our compliance team.

In accordance with 17 U.S.C. 512(c)(3), any infringement notification must include identification of the copyrighted work, proof of ownership or authorization, and your contact information.

Zero-Tracking & Complete Privacy

CineJoy is built on a privacy-first, zero-knowledge architecture:

  • No User Accounts Required: CineJoy never asks for your email address, phone number, passwords, or personal credentials.
  • 100% Client-Side Storage: Your Watchlist, Continue Watching queue, watch history, and volume/playback preferences are stored exclusively on your own device using browser localStorage.
  • Zero Tracking or Selling: We do not log IP addresses, sell analytics to third-party ad brokers, or track individual user behavior across the web.
  • Complete Data Control: You can purge all your stored data, watch history, and bookmarks at any moment by clicking "Clear All Storage" in Settings.
